SeenSooner

Live demo: four apps that share one Python API and the W2 wait-time model. All data is synthetic.

Patient App

Phone check-in and a patient's own status view. Site code: ED-MAIN.

Check-in Tablet

The same check-in on the hospital's tablet. It shows a QR slip, and patients scan it here to say they are leaving.

Nurse Dashboard

Staff queue with Remaining W2 estimates and room actions.

Waiting Room Display

Public screen for the treatment-room waiting queue.

AI Oversight Dashboard

Aggregate model monitoring for the oversight committee.
Suggested walkthrough: open the Nurse Dashboard and the Waiting Room Display in two tabs, then check in from the Patient App. The other two update live.
Synthetic demonstration only: not a clinical device and not connected to any hospital system. Mean W 34.87 → 31.75 min and LWBS 4.00% → 3.20% are hypothetical scenario projections, not observed outcomes. The first load after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.
