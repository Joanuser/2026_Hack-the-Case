SeenSooner

Live demo: patient check-in on phone or tablet, separate nurse and front desk dashboards, a waiting-room display and an AI oversight dashboard, all sharing one Python API and the W2 wait-time model. All data is synthetic.

Patient check-in with phone

Patients check in on their own phone and follow their status while they wait.

Patient check-in with tablet

The same check-in on the hospital's tablet, for patients without a phone. It prints a slip with their waiting number; later they tap “Already checked in? Tell us” here to say they are leaving or need help.

Nurse Dashboard

Nurse account: triage queue (ESI confirmed in TriageGO), Remaining W2 estimates, room calls, and same-day call-backs for higher-risk patients.

Front Desk Dashboard

Front desk account: tablet & no-phone watch list, patients not yet seen, 60-minute checks, and off-peak call-backs.

Waiting Room Display

Public screen for the treatment-room waiting queue.

AI Oversight Dashboard

Aggregate model monitoring for the oversight committee.
Suggested walkthrough: open the Nurse Dashboard, the Front Desk Dashboard and the Waiting Room Display in separate tabs, then check in with the phone or tablet. The other screens update live.
Synthetic demonstration only: not a clinical device and not connected to any hospital system. Mean W 34.87 → 31.75 min and LWBS 4.00% → 3.20% are hypothetical scenario projections, not observed outcomes. The first load after a quiet period can take up to a minute while the server wakes up.
